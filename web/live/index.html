SLOOP live

Hold a pad for a punch-in effect, ride the filter, launch and chain sections, flip presets and turn the engine's knobs from the phone, while your hands stay on the FM-1.

Plug the FM-1 into the phone with a USB data cable (an OTG adapter on most phones). Chrome on Android, or Chrome and Edge on a computer. After the first time it connects by itself when you plug it in. The pads and sections need SLOOP with protocol v11.

A new version of SLOOP live is ready.
