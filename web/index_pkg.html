Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

A groovebox you play live: four tracks — three synths and a drum machine — 12 synth engines (with six-operator FM and DX7 patches), 153 sounds, 37 drum kits from 808 to techno, amapiano and ambient, your own samples and drum kits, 27 punch-in effects. House, techno, hip-hop, drum & bass, synthwave, lo-fi, ambient: make what you want. No factory patterns, nothing to load: everything you hear, you play.
